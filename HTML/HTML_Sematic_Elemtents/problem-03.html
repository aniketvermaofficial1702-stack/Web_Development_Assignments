About CarePlus Clinic

CarePlus Multi-Specialty Clinic provides accessible healthcare services through experienced doctors and trained healthcare professionals.

Our goal is to provide safe, respectful, and patient-focused care while helping visitors understand available services and appointment procedures.

Clinic hours are Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Emergency services are not provided through this appointment portal.

How to Request an Appointment

  1. Choose a department and preferred doctor.
  2. Select a suitable date and preferred time.
  3. Complete the appointment request form.
  4. Provide accurate contact information.
  5. Wait for the clinic to confirm your appointment.

Important Terms

Consultation
A scheduled interaction with a healthcare professional regarding a patient's healthcare needs.
Follow-up
A later appointment arranged to review progress or continue previously planned care.
OPD
Outpatient Department where patients receive healthcare services without hospital admission.
Appointment
A scheduled time reserved for a consultation or healthcare service.

Patient Instructions

Please carry a valid identification document and any relevant previous medical records when visiting.

Arrive about 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment to complete registration.

If you are unable to attend, contact the clinic as early as possible so the appointment can be rescheduled.

Patient resources should be used for general information and should not replace professional medical advice.
