Product Overview

The ProBook X15 is a powerful flagship laptop designed for students, professionals and creators. It combines a high-resolution display, fast storage and a modern processor in a portable design.

With up to 32 GB RAM and 1 TB SSD, the laptop provides excellent performance for everyday productivity, coding and creative work.

Key Features

  • 15.6-inch high-resolution display
  • Latest-generation Intel Core processor
  • Up to 32 GB DDR5 RAM
  • Up to 1 TB NVMe SSD
  • Long-lasting battery
  • Backlit keyboard and precision touchpad
