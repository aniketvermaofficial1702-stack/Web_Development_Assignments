Event Overview

About the Fair

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a three-day career and education event designed for students, graduates, job seekers and professionals who want to explore new academic and career opportunities.

The fair brings together universities, technology companies, government organizations, training institutes and employers under one platform. Visitors can attend career talks, meet representatives, learn about scholarships and internships, and understand the skills required for different industries.

Event Objectives

  • Help students understand different career pathways.
  • Connect job seekers with potential employers.
  • Provide information about higher education and scholarships.
  • Introduce students to internships and apprenticeships.
  • Develop awareness about future skills and employment trends.

Preparation Guide

Before You Attend

  • Register online before visiting the venue.
  • Carry a valid student or government identification document.
  • Prepare a short introduction about yourself and your career interests.
  • Keep an updated resume ready for relevant employers.
  • Research organizations you want to meet before the event.
  • Prepare questions about courses, internships, jobs and eligibility.
  • Wear comfortable and appropriate clothing for networking.
  • Keep your registration confirmation available on your phone.

Quick Preparation Notice

Tip: Do not try to visit every organization. Select the organizations and sessions that match your goals and spend enough time having meaningful conversations.

Bring only the documents you actually need. Do not share unnecessary personal information with exhibitors.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Receive your registration confirmation.
  3. Arrive at the venue and complete entry verification.
  4. Attend your selected sessions.
  5. Meet organizations relevant to your interests.
  6. Ask questions about opportunities and eligibility.
  7. Collect useful information and follow-up contacts.
  8. Review your options and take the next career step.

Information & FAQs

Career and Education Terminology

Career Fair
An event where students and job seekers can meet employers and learn about career opportunities.
Internship
A temporary learning and work experience that helps students develop practical skills in a professional environment.
Placement
A process through which students or graduates are connected with employment opportunities.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students to support their education.
Apprenticeship
A structured program combining practical workplace training with learning and skill development.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is registration free?

Yes. Registration for the National Career & Education Fair is free for students and job seekers.

Can school students attend?

Yes. School students can attend education-focused sessions and explore future study options.

Can graduates attend?

Yes. Graduates and job seekers can meet participating employers and explore employment opportunities.

Is a resume required?

A resume is not mandatory for entry, but it is recommended for job and internship seekers.

What is the registration reference format?

NCF-2026-<REGISTRATION NUMBER>

Keep your registration reference safe because it may be required during entry and session verification.

Session capacity may be limited. A registration does not always guarantee a seat in a session unless the session is specifically confirmed.

The event covers opportunities for learners from Class 12th through postgraduate levels and working professionals.

Some technical workshops may discuss fields such as CO2 reduction, renewable energy and AI-based technologies.
