Mathematics Formula Sheet

Algebra

(a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2
(a - b)2 = a2 - 2ab + b2
a2 - b2 = (a + b)(a - b)
a2 + b2 = (a + b)2 - 2ab

Geometry

Angles

Sum of angles of a Triangle = 180°
Exterior angle = Sum of two Opposite interior angles
Sum of interior angles of a Quadrilateral = 360°
Complementary angles = 90°
Supplementary angles = 180°

Mensuration

Volume

Volume of a Cube = side3
Volume of a Cuboid = Length*Width*Height
Volume of a Sphere = 4/3*π*radius3
Volume of a Cylinder = π*radius2*Height
Volume of a Cone = 1/3*π*radius2*Height

Surface Area

Rectangle
Perimeter = 2(l + b)
Area = l × b
Square
Perimeter = 4a
Area = a²
Triangle
Area = 1/2 × Base × Height
Perimeter = a + b + c
Cuboid
TSA = 2(lb + bh + hl)
Cube
TSA = 6a²
Circle
Area= πr²
Circumference of a circle = 2π*radius

NOTE- (r-radius , l-length , b-breadth , h-height , a-side of the cube or square)
