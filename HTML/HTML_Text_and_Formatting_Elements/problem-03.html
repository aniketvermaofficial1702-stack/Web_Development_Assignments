Task Force Seeks Suggestions to Reform India’s Examination System

Date: 29 August 2026
Source: Hindustan Times

Public Suggestions for Examination Reforms

A high-powered task force headed by Infosys co-founder Nandan Nilekani has invited suggestions from students, parents, teachers and other stakeholders to improve India’s examination system. The initiative aims to make examinations more secure, transparent and student-friendly.

Areas of Reforms

The task force is seeking opinions on several areas, including exam security, technology, governance and student well-being. Students and other stakeholders can submit their suggestions in English, Hindi or regional languages. The deadline for submitting suggestions is 13 September 2026.

Concerns About the Examination System

The move comes amid growing concerns about the examination system, including issues related to leaks, security and the pressure faced by students. The government hopes that involving students, teachers and parents directly will help identify practical problems and develop better solutions.

Importance for Students

This initiative is important because examinations play a major role in determining students' academic and career opportunities. A reliable and transparent examination system could reduce stress among students and increase public confidence in the results.

Conclusion

The proposed reforms could be an important step toward creating a fairer and more modern examination system in India. Taking suggestions directly from students and educators can help ensure that future reforms address the real challenges faced by those who participate in examinations.

Editor’s Note

This article highlights the need to improve India’s examination system by making it more secure, transparent, and student-friendly. The involvement of students, teachers, and parents in suggesting reforms is a positive step toward creating a fair and reliable education system.
