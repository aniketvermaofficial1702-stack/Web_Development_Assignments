Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra


Overview

Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra is Samsung'spremium flagship smartphone , designed for users who want performance, advanced photography, AI features, and productivity tools. It combines a large high-quality display, a powerful processor, an advanced camera system, a long-lasting battery, and an integrated S Pen.

It is designed to deliver a premium smartphone experience for entertainment, photography, gaming, productivity, and everyday use.

Key Features

Display: 6.9-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 3200 x 1440 pixels, 120Hz refresh rate
Processor: Exynos 2400 / Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 optimized for Galaxy devices.
Camera System: Quad camera setup with a 200MP main camera, ultra-wide lens, telephoto lens, and periscope zoom lens
Battery: 5000mAh with fast charging and wireless charging support
S Pen Integration: Built-in S Pen for note-taking, drawing, and productivity features.
Software: Android 15 with Samsung One UI 6.0
Connectivity: 5G support, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4

Special Information

The Galaxy S26 Ultra is Samsung's top-end Galaxy S-series smartphone. Its major highlights include Galaxy AI, advanced cameras, powerful performance, fast charging, and S Pen integration .

Samsung has also focused on thermal management to help maintain performance during demanding activities such as gaming and video recording.

The smartphone is available in several colours, including Violet, Sky Blue, Black, and White, with additional colours depending on the market.

Important: The charging adapter is sold separately in many markets; check the package contents before purchasing.
