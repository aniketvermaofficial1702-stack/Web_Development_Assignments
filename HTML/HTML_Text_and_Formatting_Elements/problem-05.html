SCIENCE NOTES

PHYSICS

Powers & Units:
1 km = 103 m
1 cm = 10-2 m
1 mm = 10-3m
1 m = 103 mm
1 m = 102 cm

Important Equations:
v = u + at
s = ut + 1/2at2
v2 = u2 + 2as

Nuclear Fusion:
2H + 3H → 4He + 1n + Energy

CHEMISTRY

Burning of carbon:
C + O2 → CO2
Burning of magnesium:
2Mg + O2 → 2MgO
Burning of sulphur:
S + O2 → SO2
Displacement Reactions:
Fe + CuSO4 → FeSO4 + Cu
Zn + CuSO4 → ZnSO4 + Cu

Oxidation & Reduction :
Oxidation : Loss of electrons / Gain of oxygen
Reduction : Gain of electrons / Loss of oxygen
Redox : Oxidation + Reduction occurring together

BIOLOGY


Photosynthesis:
6CO2 + 6H2O + Light Energy → C6H12O6 + 6O2
Respiration:
C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + Energy
Lactic Acid Fermentation:
C6H12O6 → 2C3H6O3 + Energy

Basic Biological Processes:
Digestion : Complex food → Simple soluble substances
Respiration : Glucose → Energy
Photosynthesis : CO₂ + H₂O → Glucose + O₂
Excretion : Removal of metabolic waste
Transpiration : Loss of water vapour from plants
