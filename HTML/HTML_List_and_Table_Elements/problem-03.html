Practical Class Sequence

  1. Attend the lecture.
  2. Read the practical instructions.
  3. Complete the assigned experiment.
  4. Record observations.
  5. Submit the practical work.

Academic Terms

  • Lecture: Teacher-led theoretical session.
  • Lab: Practical hands-on session.
  • Tutorial: Guided problem-solving session.
  • Internal Assessment: Periodic academic evaluation.
  • Practical: Application-based laboratory work.
