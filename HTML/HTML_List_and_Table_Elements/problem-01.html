1. Introduction

Welcome to the GLA University Admission Handbook. This handbook provides essential information about the admission process, eligibility requirements, required documents, important admission terms, fee structure, and contact details for prospective students.

2.Admission Overview

GLA University offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various disciplines including Engineering, Management, Computer Applications, Sciences, Commerce, and Humanities.

The admission process is designed to help students complete registration, document verification, counselling, fee payment, and final admission smoothly.

Key Admission Information

Key Admission Information
  • University: GLA University
  • Location: Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, India
  • Admission Type: Undergraduate and Postgraduate
  • Application Mode: Online and Offline
  • Academic Session: 2026–27
  • Admission Basis: Merit and applicable entrance examinations
  • Official Website: GLA University official website

3. Eligibility

General Eligibility Requirements

  • Candidates must have passed the required qualifying examination.
  • Candidates must satisfy the minimum percentage requirements for their selected program.
  • Candidates must meet the subject requirements applicable to their chosen course.
  • Valid entrance examination scores may be required for selected programs.
  • Candidates must provide valid academic and identity documents during verification.

For B.Tech Applicants

  • Must have completed Class 12 or an equivalent examination.
  • Must have studied the required subjects, generally including Physics and Mathematics.
  • The third subject requirement depends on the admission rules of the program.
  • Admission may be based on qualifying examination/entrance examination performance as applicable.

4. Application Process

Use these as your ordered steps:
  1. Visit the official university admission portal.
  2. Create a new student account.
  3. Enter the required personal and academic information.
  4. Select the desired program or course.
  5. Upload the required documents.
  6. Review all information entered in the application form.
  7. Submit the application form.
  8. Pay the applicable application or registration fee.
  9. Complete counselling or document verification, if required.
  10. Receive the admission confirmation.
  11. Pay the applicable admission/tuition fee.
  12. Complete the final registration process.

5. Required Documents

These are your unordered-list items, because the documents do not have to be presented in a particular sequence.
  • Class 10 marksheet and certificate
  • Class 12 marksheet and certificate
  • Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Migration Certificate, if applicable
  • Character Certificate
  • Entrance examination scorecard, if applicable
  • Aadhaar Card or other valid identity proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Category certificate, if applicable
  • Income certificate, if applicable
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Admission/registration form
  • Fee payment receipt

Important : Students should carry original documents as well as the required photocopies during verification.

6. Important Terms

Eligibility
The conditions a student must satisfy to be considered for admission to a particular program.
Merit List
A list of eligible applicants arranged according to their academic performance or applicable admission criteria.
Counselling
A process in which eligible students receive guidance regarding courses, branches, seats, admission procedures, and other academic options.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students according to specified scholarship criteria.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling a student in the university or a particular academic program.
Document Verification
The process of checking the authenticity and validity of documents submitted by an applicant.
Tuition Fee
The amount charged by the university for providing academic instruction and educational services.
Admission Confirmation
Official confirmation that a student's admission has been successfully completed.
